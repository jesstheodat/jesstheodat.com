About & Philosophy

Systems Mindset

True reliability isn't the absence of failure—it's the capacity to handle complexity and unexpected friction without breaking what matters.

I work at the intersection of site reliability engineering, infrastructure security, and systems architecture. At Google, my focus spans high-availability architecture, on-premises compute, network and storage security, and insider-risk reduction.

Beyond enterprise scale, my personal engineering work is an extension of this exact same systems mindset. Through self-hosted infrastructure, local home automation, and custom hardware controllers, I explore how dependencies can be minimized, risks reduced, and resilience built directly into the technology we use every day.

As an emerging strategist in digital privacy and defensive exposure reduction, I help individuals and organizations audit their exposure, harden their digital footprint, and regain intentional control over their data in an increasingly interconnected world.
